# Specialty / genre recipes

Decade-coded styles. Not offered by the Direction Advisor - developers arrive already naming them. **Commit fully**: half-genre reads as a broken modern site. Shared rules: [../design-directions.md](../design-directions.md).

---

## y2k-retrofuturism - Y2K / Frutiger Aero

- **Vibe:** 1999-2005 techno-optimism - chrome, glassy bubbles, jelly translucency, lens flare. **Best for:** fashion, music, nostalgia-coded products, playful campaigns. **Touchstone:** iMac G3 ads, Windows Vista Aero, aqua-era buttons, MySpace pages.
- **Palette:** ground ice blue `#A3D5FF` or chrome silver `#E0E4EA`; iridescent glass cyan `#5FC9F8` → magenta `#FF66C4`; brushed-chrome highlights `#C4C9D2` → `#F2F4F8`; acid green `#A8FF00` pops, sparingly; ink black or white depending on ground.
- **Type:** chunky rounded sans with personality (Frutiger, Eurostile, heavy Sharp Grotesk), sometimes chrome-treated; body Frutiger or Avenir 14-16px; LCD-style numerals for counters; underline-plus-sparkle link hovers.
- **Spacing:** packed but bubbly - 4 / 8 / 16 / 24.
- **Radius:** gummy - 16 / 24 / 32 / full pill. **Shadow:** heavy and colored, e.g. `0 16px 32px rgba(95,201,248,0.4)`, plus inner top highlights to fake glass.
- **Motion:** bouncy springs, glow pulses, lens-flare sweeps - early-Flash energy.
- **Signature moves:** translucent jelly buttons with an inner top highlight; oil-slick iridescent gradients; chrome type with sparkles; specular highlights on every glass surface; dimensional dock-like UI; slowly drifting orbs and bubbles.
- **Avoid:** sharp corners; earthy or muted palettes; restrained typography; anything resembling tasteful 2020s minimalism.
- **Prompt seed:** chrome silver and ice-blue iridescent background, floating glass orbs with cyan/magenta reflections, lens flare upper right, one glossy translucent pill button, late-90s optimism, 16:9.
- **Don't use when:** no retro or playful claim; enterprise/professional audiences; long-form reading.

## mid-century-modern - Paul Rand / Saul Bass

- **Vibe:** 1950s-60s American graphic modernism; geometric shapes; jazz-sleeve confidence. **Best for:** cultural, film, and publishing identities; heritage consumer brands. **Touchstone:** Rand's IBM logo, Bass's film posters, Blue Note sleeves.
- **Palette:** ground cream `#EBE3D2`, mustard `#D9A441`, or muted teal `#3D6E70`; ink `#1A1A18`; three colors max per piece (one bold, two muted) - e.g. mustard + brick + cream, teal + cream + black, sienna + ink + bone.
- **Type:** geometric grotesque (Futura, Avenir, Avant Garde), often condensed, at large scale - or an Egyptian slab for editorial moments; humanist serif body (Sabon, Garamond) 16-17px; a single huge numeral used as a graphic.
- **Spacing:** structured grid - 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48. **Radius:** 0. **Shadow:** none - flat shapes only.
- **Motion:** minimal; shapes slide into place on a single beat, like a title sequence.
- **Signature moves:** one big geometric shape (circle, triangle, half-moon) as the main visual; slightly imperfect "cut paper" edges; type integrated with shapes (curving around a circle, sitting inside a block); one repeated stylized motif; low-opacity paper grain.
- **Avoid:** gradients; photography as the lead (illustration leads); a fourth color; rounded UI.
- **Prompt seed:** Saul Bass-style poster, one large mustard `#D9A441` circle on cream `#EBE3D2` paper, flat shapes, faint paper texture, hand-cut edges, no gradient, no shadow, 3:4.
- **Don't use when:** feature-dense utility products; no heritage or craft claim; realistic photography is essential.
