# Warm humanist recipes

Approachable and hand-touched; says real people made this for real people. Most of these depend on genuine illustration or photography - if it can't be sourced, say so. Shared rules: [../design-directions.md](../design-directions.md).

---

## mailchimp-freddie - Mailchimp (Freddie era)

- **Vibe:** hand-drawn marks, warm yellow, personality in the microcopy. **Best for:** small-business and creator tools, education, community, approachable B2C. **Touchstone:** pre-2023 mailchimp.com.
- **Palette:** brand yellow `#FFE01B` for hero/brand moments, white for body; ink `#241C15` (never pure black); cream `#FBEFE3`; secondary `#88837C`; hot pink `#FF4D74` or warm coral, sparingly; **three colors visible at once max**.
- **Type:** friendly grotesque (Helvetica Now Display, Söhne, Inter Tight) 700-800 display; body same family 17-18px / 1.6; occasional hand-lettered touches.
- **Spacing:** 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 - generous, not airy.
- **Radius:** 8 / 16 / 24; pill buttons. **Shadow:** warm `0 4px 12px rgba(36,28,21,0.08)`.
- **Motion:** bouncy is welcome - spring hover lifts, friendly easing.
- **Signature moves:** characterful hand-drawn illustration from a single illustrator's hand (commissioned, not generated); yellow flood-filled sections breaking up the page; pill buttons with a 2-3px black outline; human microcopy ("Send better email", not "Optimize delivery metrics"); tilted, asymmetric illustration placement.
- **Avoid:** pure black; stock illustration; more than three colors on screen; cold corporate copy.
- **Prompt seed:** hand-drawn line character with warm yellow and coral washes, winking, on cream `#FBEFE3`, deliberate imperfection, 4:5.
- **Don't use when:** premium/luxury positioning; no real illustrator available (loses half its impact); skeptical enterprise audiences.

## stripe-press - Stripe Press

- **Vibe:** books as objects; cream and ink; warm photography; unhurried. **Best for:** publishing, longform, "books and ideas" products. **Touchstone:** press.stripe.com, the cloth-bound books.
- **Palette:** ground `#F1ECDE` (warm bone); darker surface `#E6DCC4`; ink `#1A1A18`; secondary `#736D5A`; one foil-stamp accent per book or chapter - deep teal `#1B4B5A` or burnt sienna `#A04A2A`, never combined; hairline `#C8BEA4`.
- **Type:** refined transitional/modern serif display (GT Sectra, Domaine Display) with italics everywhere; old-style body serif (Source Serif, Lyon Text, Galliard) 18-19px / 1.65; humanist sans (Söhne, GT America) only for small UI text; wide display serif with slim body serif.
- **Spacing:** 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96.
- **Radius:** 0 - books don't have rounded corners. **Shadow:** on book objects only, `0 24px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.18)`.
- **Motion:** slow - 1-2s crossfades; books rotate gently (≤15°) in 3D on hover; ~20s Ken Burns on hero photos.
- **Signature moves:** real book-cover photography on cream with raking light and an honest shadow as the hero; oversized italic serif emphasis; author/chapter quotes hung in a wide margin; faint cloth texture for section breaks; one foil color per book.
- **Avoid:** pure white grounds; sans body; book card grids (each book is a feature); cool grays.
- **Prompt seed:** hardcover book on warm cream `#F1ECDE`, raking window light, deep soft shadow, cloth spine visible, title foil-stamped in burnt sienna, 4:5.
- **Don't use when:** no editorial or publication content; light or jokey voices; audiences who won't read longform.

## headspace-meditation - Headspace / Calm

- **Vibe:** soft pastels, rounded everything, calming illustration. **Best for:** wellness, meditation, sleep, health, gentle expert services. **Touchstone:** headspace.com and app.
- **Palette:** ground warm peach `#FFE2C5` or `#FFEDD5`; darker surface coral `#F4A573`; ink dark teal `#1B3A47`; secondary `#5C6B7A`; rotating pop accents lavender `#B0A5D1`, sage `#9DB67A`, salmon `#F5867B` - one per scene; two colors per scene max.
- **Type:** rounded friendly sans (Apercu, rounded GT America, Söhne Breit) 600 display; body 16-17px 400; an occasional soft handwritten line (e.g. Caveat) for emotional beats only.
- **Spacing:** generous - 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96.
- **Radius:** 16 / 24 / 32; pill buttons. **Shadow:** soft, tinted to the ground, e.g. `0 8px 24px rgba(244,165,115,0.2)`.
- **Motion:** gentle and slow, 400-800ms; buoyant hover lifts; subtle 1.5-4s breathing cycles on key elements; no bouncy springs.
- **Signature moves:** a simple round mascot character; breathing circles that expand and contract with the copy; peach gradient grounds that drift slowly; every container a pill or soft rectangle; calm microcopy ("Take a moment. We'll wait.").
- **Avoid:** cool palettes (even night mode stays warm teal, not navy); sharp corners; jolting motion; more than two colors per scene.
- **Prompt seed:** flat illustration on warm peach `#FFE2C5`, one round coral mascot with a friendly face, soft sage and lavender shapes, no hard edges, gentle imperfection, 4:5.
- **Don't use when:** sharp/sophisticated positioning; no care or wellness angle (feels infantilizing); authoritative expert voices.
