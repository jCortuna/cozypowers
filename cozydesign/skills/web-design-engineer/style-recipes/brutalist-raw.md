# Brutalist / raw recipes

Ugly-on-purpose reads as authentic among polished defaults. **Commit fully** - half-measures look broken, not bold. Shared rules: [../design-directions.md](../design-directions.md).

---

## are-na - Are.na (the honest web)

- **Vibe:** system fonts on purpose; content over chrome; the early web, considered. **Best for:** indie tools, creative platforms, research-flavored products, artist communities. **Touchstone:** are.na.
- **Palette:** ground `#FFFFFF`; ink `#000000`; browser-default link blue `#0000EE` and visited purple `#551A8B`, used sincerely; hairline `#CCCCCC`; optional cream card surface `#F4F1E8`.
- **Type:** system serif (Times/Georgia) and/or the OS default sans; no web fonts - let the browser choose; body 14-16px / ~1.45; underlined links.
- **Spacing:** tight and dense - 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32. **Radius:** 0. **Shadow:** none. **Motion:** none.
- **Signature moves:** default underlined blue links with purple visited state; headings that are just bigger body type; tables, lists, and prose flowing without ornament; tiny plain link-heavy footers; any cards are flat rectangles with a 1px gray border.
- **Avoid:** custom fonts; any color beyond links, ink, ground; hover transitions; modals, tooltips, micro-interactions; polish of any kind.
- **Prompt seed:** found-photo collage on plain white paper, mixed sources, no retouching, hand-cut feel, 4:3.
- **Don't use when:** premium positioning; audiences expecting modern SaaS cues (they'll think it's broken); anyone tempted to half-commit.

## bloomberg-businessweek-turley - Businessweek (Turley era)

- **Vibe:** typographic violence; the magazine grid abused; copy as image. **Best for:** editorial features, agency portfolios, opinionated content. **Touchstone:** Businessweek covers 2014-2019.
- **Palette:** alert orange `#FF3D00`, signal yellow `#FFE800`, alert blue `#001AFF`, on white `#FFFFFF` or inverted on black; black text where legible; **three colors max per spread**.
- **Type:** one bold grotesque at absurd scale (Founders Grotesk, Druk, ABC Whyte) 800-900 at 200px+, broken across lines and kerned uncomfortably tight, often condensed all-caps; tiny 12-14px serif body (Plantin, Mercury) for contrast.
- **Spacing:** intentionally broken - off-grid, touching edges, overflowing. **Radius:** 0. **Shadow:** none.
- **Motion:** hard cuts; anything that moves slams into place.
- **Signature moves:** headlines that visibly break the grid and hit the page edge; saturated color blocks behind type; awkwardly cropped photo cutouts dropped on color fields; data tables styled as bold opinionated spreads; tiny italic serif captions in unexpected corners.
- **Avoid:** tastefulness; a fourth color; smooth transitions; anything that looks generated rather than set.
- **Prompt seed:** magazine cover, one word in massive black grotesque over flat alert-orange `#FF3D00`, a clumsily cut photo silhouette at an odd angle, slight print misregistration, 3:4.
- **Don't use when:** calm or trustworthy voices; utility content; teams uneasy with deliberate ugliness.

## balenciaga-post-2017 - Balenciaga (post-2017)

- **Vibe:** default browser styling as a weapon; Helvetica at absurd scale; anti-luxury luxury. **Best for:** fashion, fragrance, counter-culture brands. **Touchstone:** 2018+ balenciaga.com snapshots.
- **Palette:** black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, one off-white `#F5F4F0`. No color in the UI - ever. Color exists only inside photographs.
- **Type:** Helvetica or Arial only, any weight or size, never another family; all-caps hero headlines 140px+, tightly tracked, hard against the left edge; body 11-13px.
- **Spacing:** 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 - restrained, "didn't bother to soften it" rather than spacious. **Radius:** 0. **Shadow:** none.
- **Motion:** none; hover changes an underline at most.
- **Signature moves:** giant tight all-caps Helvetica; harshly lit, awkwardly posed editorial fashion photography (never smiling stock); navigation as plain text links, sometimes a vertical list; minimal metadata captions; CTAs as underlined text, never styled buttons.
- **Avoid:** any other font; color; polished affordances (rounded buttons, shadows, hover effects); decoration.
- **Prompt seed:** fashion editorial, one model against plain off-white `#F5F4F0`, harsh direct flash, static awkward pose, deadpan, 4:5.
- **Don't use when:** no fashion or counter-culture claim (cosplay); audiences expecting to be sold to; conversion-critical pages.
