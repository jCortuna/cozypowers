# Credits

cozydesign is a clean-room, markdown-only recreation of design skills featured in the AI LABS video *"Insane Claude Design Skills You Need To Actually Build Beautiful Sites"*. Every skill here was rewritten in new words and condensed; executable scripts, JavaScript/CSS starter assets, and promotional text from the originals were deliberately left out, and script-driven steps were replaced with procedures the agent follows by hand.

The ideas, structure, and many specific values (easing curves, spacing tokens, contrast thresholds, style recipes) come from the authors below. All original works are MIT licensed. If you like a skill, go read and star the original.

| cozydesign skill | Based on | Author | Source |

| `design-engineering` | `emil-design-eng` | Emil Kowalski | https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills |
| `animate` | `animate` | Emil Kowalski | https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills |
| `web-design-engineer` | `web-design-engineer` (incl. critique guide, failure patterns, style recipes) | ConardLi | https://github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills |
| `landing-page-design` | `landing-page-design` | Elaya | https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills |
| `video-to-superprompt` | `video-to-superprompt` | Meng To | https://github.com/MengTo/Skills |
| `awwwards-quality-sites` | `build-awwwards-quality-sites` | Meng To | https://github.com/MengTo/Skills |
