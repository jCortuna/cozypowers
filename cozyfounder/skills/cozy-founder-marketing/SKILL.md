# cozy-founder-marketing

A business no one hears about does not exist. This skill plans how the first
customers hear about it, from what the pack already learned rather than a generic
playbook.

## Step 1: read the evidence

- `founder/panel/results.md`: who buys and why not. The highest-buying segments
  are the first audience; the top objections are what the marketing must answer.
- `founder/competitors.md`: how competitors position themselves (so you do not
  sound like them) and the gap.
- `founder/offer.md`, `founder/pricing.md`: what is sold, at what price.
- `founder/cfo.md`: contribution per unit, which caps what a customer may cost.

If these are missing, say what is missing and offer a quick
`cozy-founder-consumer` run first. Do not invent an audience.

## Step 2: positioning

One line, then the reasons:

```
For <the segment that buys most>, who <their problem in their words>,
<business> is the <category> that <the gap>, unlike <the nearest alternative>.
```

Write three versions, recommend one, and cite the panel evidence behind it.

## Step 3: channels

Where the first audience really spends attention, specific to the business and
place: foot traffic and window signs, map listings, short video, local creators,
neighbourhood groups, partnerships with nearby businesses, email, paid search,
paid social. For each: why it fits this buyer, rough cost, and how you will know
it worked. Pick two or three; say what you are not doing and why.

## Step 4: the 30-day campaign

`founder/marketing.md` gets a dated calendar from launch minus 14 days to launch
plus 16: what goes out each day, on which channel, with the message. Include:

- **Ten hooks**, each answering one panel objection or using one buyer reason,
  written for the format (a three-second video opener, an ad headline, a window
  sign).
- The launch offer from `founder/pricing.md` and when it ends.
- What to post on days with nothing new (behind the scenes, the making of).

## Step 5: budget and numbers

- The budget split across the chosen channels.
- The most you can pay to win a customer: contribution per unit × purchases per
  buyer in the period you choose, with the sum shown.
- Three numbers to watch weekly and the level that means "change something".

## Rules

- No fake reviews, follower counts, testimonials, before-and-afters or "as seen on".
- Panel quotes are research; never use them as customer quotes in ads.
- Disclose paid partnerships. Follow the user's market's rules on claims (health
  and earnings especially). Not legal advice.

## Output

`founder/marketing.md`. Next: `cozy-founder-brand`, then `cozy-founder-launch`.
