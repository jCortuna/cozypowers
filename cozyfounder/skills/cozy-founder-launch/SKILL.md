# cozy-founder-launch

A launch is a project with a date. This skill turns the rest of the pack into a
dated plan, and starts with the cheapest way to learn whether real people will
pay before the big money goes out.

## Step 1: test before you spend

Pick the cheapest real test that fits, and run it before committing the big costs
in `founder/numbers.json`:

| business | the test |
| --- | --- |
| physical shop or café | a pop-up, a market stall or a pre-order list with a price and a date |
| online product | a landing page with the price and a pre-order or waitlist button |
| service or agency | three paid pilot clients at the planned price |
| subscription | a founding-member offer, paid up front |

Write the success line before running it (for example: 50 pre-orders in two weeks
at $6.50) and compare the result with the panel's buy rate. If real buyers fall
well short of the panel, trust the real buyers and go back to
`cozy-founder-offer` or `cozy-founder-pricing`.

## Step 2: the countdown

From today to launch day, with real dates. Ask for the target date; otherwise
default to the earliest realistic one given the lead times in `founder/ops.md`.
Weekly blocks, each task with an owner and a deadline:

- Legal and money: registration, bank account, permits from `founder/ops.md`.
- Build: fit-out or website; suppliers ordered by their lead times.
- Brand: sign, packaging, profiles from `founder/brand.md`.
- Marketing: the calendar in `founder/marketing.md` from launch minus 14 days.
- Team: hiring and training on the routines.
- A soft open or friends-and-family day before the public launch.

Mark the critical path: the tasks that move the launch date if they slip.

## Step 3: launch day

An hour-by-hour run sheet: who is where, what goes out on which channel, the
opening offer, and what to do if something breaks (from the risk register).

## Step 4: the first 30 days

The weekly numbers to track (units a day against break-even from the CFO; the
channel numbers from marketing), the level that triggers a change, and reviews on
days 7, 14 and 30 with the questions to answer.

## Output

`founder/launch.md` with the test, the countdown, the run sheet and the 30-day
review. Next: `cozy-founder-plan` compiles everything into the business plan.
